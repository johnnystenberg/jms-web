The full tax breakdown

🇸🇪 Sweden

  • Municipal & regional tax, national income tax
  • Basic allowance and earned income tax credit
  • Burial fee, optional church fee
  • Tax-table simulation and higher allowances for 66+

🇩🇰 Denmark

  • AM-bidrag, bundskat, mellemskat & topskat
  • Top-top tax (new in 2026), kommuneskat, kirkeskat
  • Beskæftigelsesfradrag & personfradrag, ATP, tax ceiling

🇬🇧 United Kingdom

  • Income Tax, including Scottish rates
  • National Insurance
  • Student loan plans 1–5 and postgraduate
  • Personal-allowance taper

🇩🇪 Germany

  • Lohnsteuer (§32a), solidarity surcharge, church tax
  • Health, pension, unemployment and long-term-care insurance
  • Tax class I–VI, federal state and children
